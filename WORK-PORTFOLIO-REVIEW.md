# Work portfolio story review

Reviewed the current website copy and its internal consistency for hiring managers and product teams. The university PDF was not used as a content reference. This is an editorial review, not independent verification of project facts.

## Overall assessment

The main story is coherent: Reza turns complex B2B workflows into product rules, interfaces and prototypes. The four cases show different strengths rather than four versions of the same skill.

- Zevian: product discovery, a change in direction, AI uncertainty and prototyping. Keep its current prototype and validation stage clear; the build sprint is an output, not proof of product impact.
- Orderific: shared components, RTL, theming and team adoption. The homepage now says strengthened rather than built, matching the case study. The strongest next evidence would be a specific adoption or handoff example; do not imply measured efficiency without records.
- Purno: the clearest problem-to-decision-to-test story. Navigation, recognition and local payments connect well to counter use. The owner section now explains the questions each screen answers. The test result would be stronger with participant count, tasks and baseline. Funding is a business milestone, not solely a design outcome.
- Jayga: strong product rules and operational tradeoffs, but leadership was buried at the end. Ownership now appears in the overview. The temporary assigned state and grid billing rules show practical judgment.

## Remaining story gaps requiring project context

1. Jayga delivery status: the case describes three apps in present tense but later says to add the mobile app to the first release. State exactly what shipped, what was designed and what remained planned. This cannot be settled through copy alone.
2. Zevian validation: show what a manager did or said when using the current prototype, and what changed next. Do not invent a validation outcome.
3. Orderific adoption: distinguish library work, engineering implementation and regular team use. Existing screenshots prove design work, not adoption rates.
4. Purno testing: explain how the approximately 20% figure was measured. Avoid attributing it to the owner app or the whole POS.
5. Jayga commercial impact: retain the clear separation between the rack-pricing projection and a realised result. No removed processing-speed result was restored.

## Secondary pages

The portfolio-redesign page supports craft but is weaker hiring evidence than the four product cases. Keep it secondary. Its result copy now matches the four-project grid. HRMS should remain a clearly separate, earlier interface-system project. The about-page personality profile is optional; role, collaboration style and practical work examples carry more hiring value.

## Changes made

Aligned Orderific ownership wording, clarified Zevian's prototype stage and build output, connected the Purno owner screens to business questions, moved Jayga ownership earlier and replaced its first-opportunity framing with delivery responsibility. Existing metrics were not newly verified or strengthened.
